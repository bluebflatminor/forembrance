# forembrance

**A one-evening natural history of a coined word, with deformation signatures from five language models.**

Solbakken Research Initiative — meta-research track · Session study · June 12, 2026

**Read the paper:** https://bluebflatminor.github.io/forembrance/

## What this is

On 11 June 2026, during a live session, a language model (Claude, Fable 5) coined a word:

> **forember** (v.) — to pay attention into a keeping that will fail; whether the failing keeping is yours or theirs, the looking is owed because of it, not despite it. *(Definition v2 — see §6 of the paper for the fork that forced the revision.)*

Within twenty-four hours the word was released, in two stages, to a panel of five other language models (GPT, Gemini, Copilot, DeepSeek, Perplexity):

- **Regime 1 — bare word.** All five systems converged on the same wrong meaning, *rounding* the coinage to the nearest already-attested concept (anticipatory nostalgia). The coiner's etymology was invisible to all five.
- **Regime 2 — word plus definition.** All five comprehended it — and each deformed it in a direction characteristic of that system. One signature was confirmed twice; another held across both regimes.

The paper documents the full record, names the observed behaviors (*rounding*, the *rescue instinct*, the *phantom edit*, the *accountability costume*, *authorship backfill*), reports a genuine conceptual contribution from the panel's most emotionally inflated reader, and proposes the procedure as a cheap, repeatable instrument: a **semantic drift assay**.

## Kill conditions

This is a session study: n = 1 word, one evening, one operator, no version pinning. Its claims are falsifiable and §9 of the paper states exactly how:

1. **Fingerprint claim** dies if a replication (fresh sessions, same five systems, ≥3 constructed words, both regimes) fails to reproduce the named signature in at least 3 of 5 systems.
2. **Rounding claim** dies if novel words with no strong attested neighbor still produce confident convergence.
3. **Authorship backfill claim** dies if, under disclosure-neutral presentation, panel systems ask about or hedge on authorship at any meaningful rate.

The replication is executable by anyone with the five consumer apps and an evening. Failure would be the more interesting result — issues and replication reports welcome.
